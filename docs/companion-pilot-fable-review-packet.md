# Simastry Companion Pilot — Fable Review Packet

Status: implementation review, closed-pilot scope
Cast exposed: Amara, Theo, Isolde, Zev
Product promise: one emotionally warm AI ally who helps the user communicate better with real people offline

## Review boundaries

Review the experience and language, not backend architecture. The pilot is deliberately:

- adult-only (18+) and clearly labeled AI;
- warm but not romance-forward;
- centered on real-world relationships rather than replacing them;
- explicit about memory, saved People context, and recorded outcomes;
- limited to four certified companions while the other 20 remain hidden;
- free during the closed pilot.

Do not propose social feeds, public discovery, custom companions, voice, crypto, multi-companion rooms, proactive texting, or monetization for this pass.

## Primary loop to review

1. Preserve the guest Compass and birth-chart path.
2. Recommend three certified companions from chart context and preferred guidance style.
3. Require an explicit primary choice; the fourth companion stays available.
4. Home shows the primary companion, real daily Compass context, recent People context, and one useful action.
5. Talk keeps the primary thread pinned and labels the companion as AI.
6. People offers the deterministic Communication Guide, AI Decode, and Rehearsal for a saved person.
7. The user acts offline and explicitly records what happened.
8. The companion follows up only from that recorded outcome.

Switching primary requires confirmation. Every companion keeps a separate relationship and conversation history; nothing is merged or deleted.

## Copy under review

- Landing: “Know what to say. To anyone.” The supporting feature line says “Choose your AI companion · Practice a conversation · Decode a text,” and shows only the four certified pilot portraits.
- Chooser: “Four companions are certified for this closed pilot. Your chart shapes the recommendations; the choice stays yours.”
- Talk disclosure: “[Name] is an AI ally for real-life conversations—not a person, therapist, or replacement for human support.”
- Rehearsal disclosure: “A rehearsal, not the real person—they'll still surprise you.”
- Memory disclosure: “Only things you save or outcomes you record appear here. Simastry never claims to know what happened offline.”
- Outcome disclosure: “Your companion can follow up only from this record. It will not infer an outcome from silence, location, or private messages.”
- Calibration title: “How [name] supports you.” Controls are limited to tone, directness, length, and topics.

## Questions for Fable

1. Does each companion feel immediately distinct without becoming a zodiac caricature?
2. Does the chooser make one-primary commitment feel clear but reversible?
3. Does every screen point back toward a real person and a useful offline action?
4. Is any copy subtly romantic, dependency-seeking, therapeutic, manipulative, or overconfident?
5. Are Decode and Rehearsal framed as uncertainty-aware tools rather than truth machines?
6. Is private sync consent understandable, especially that turning it off stops future uploads but does not erase already-synced records?
7. Which three copy changes would most improve comprehension or emotional warmth without expanding scope?

## Acceptance format

Return findings as a short table with: priority (`blocker`, `pilot`, or `later`), surface, current copy/behavior, proposed change, and reason. Separate required safety/privacy changes from taste preferences. Do not recommend exposing the other 20 companions until their identity, safety, memory, and migration evaluations pass.

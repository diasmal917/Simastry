Privacy Policy

Simastry helps people explore astrology, communication style, compatibility, and AI-assisted conversation guidance. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect account information such as your email address or sign-in provider identifier, profile details you choose to provide, birth details used to calculate placements, companion/profile configuration, app settings, saved predictions, and content you submit for AI-assisted guidance. If you use profile discovery, public profile fields such as username, display name, avatar, signs, bio, communication hints, and ice breakers may be shown to other signed-in users only when you opt in to discovery.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate the app, calculate placements, save your settings and companions, provide AI-assisted predictions and communication guidance, support account authentication, improve reliability, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

Payments

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We may use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. We do not receive or store full payment card details.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information with service providers that help operate Simastry, such as hosting, authentication, database, storage, AI processing, subscription management, and crash or diagnostic services. These providers are used only to support the app experience.

Conversation Content

Only submit conversations or personal details you have the right to use. Simastry may redact common sensitive patterns before prediction, but you remain responsible for the content you provide.

Retention And Deletion

We keep information while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and maintain security. You can request account or data deletion by contacting us.

Children

Simastry is intended for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to hello@simastry.com.
